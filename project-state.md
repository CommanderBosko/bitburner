Moved the decision into `controller.ts` (`decideActiveWorkScript`, +1.0GB: `ns.kill` and `ns.singularity.getCurrentWork`, confirmed live at exactly 12.50GB). Behavior: `crime-loop.js` exclusively pre-gang; post-gang it's killed **permanently** (not idled), `augment-loop.js` runs unconditionally (doesn't touch the work slot), and faction/company are mutually exclusive — faction preferred, falls back to company after a 90s grace period if `getCurrentWork()` shows no real faction work happening, re-probes faction every 5 minutes while on company. Confirmed live: `ps` shows only `crime-loop.js` resident pre-gang (not faction/company), `mem controller.js` = 12.50GB exactly as predicted, `tail crime-loop.js` actively committing Homicide with no errors.
